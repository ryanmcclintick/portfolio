ABOUT ME

Mechanical engineer, driven by curiosity.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at UCLA who likes understanding how things work, figuring out how to make them better, and most importantly building something tangible along the way.

WHO I AM

I like understanding how things work, then making them better.

My interests have taken me across mechanical design, mechatronics, experimentation, and human-centered engineering. Recently, that has led me to research haptics and human perception with the Mechatronics for Outreach, Haptics, Agriculture, and Living Applications (MOHALA) Lab at UCLA, where I'm exploring how people interact with and perceive physical feedback.

Additionally, I currently am a sub-team lead for the Bruin Underwater Robotics (BUR) Club at UCLA, overseeing the creation of a biomimetic tail for the propulsion of a fish-inspired autonomous underwater robot. BUR competes in RoboSub, an international student competition focused on the design and construction of robotic submarines. BUR has given me the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in project management, semi-compliant design, prototyping, and modeling.

Previously, I worked as a Mechanical Engineering Intern for Emisense, an ultrafine particulate matter sensor company working out of UCR’s Bourns College of Engineering - Center for Environmental Research & Technology (CE-CERT). Through my time with them, I gained invaluable experience in 3D Design, fluid mechanics, and manufacturing processes while learning how to work and operate in professional, team-focused environments.

Engineering is only one part of what I do.

Some of the experiences that have shaped me most have happened outside the classroom.

I'm a member of the executive board for UCLA's Men's Club Water Polo Team, helping coordinate practices, fundraising, social media, alumni relations, and team-bonding. Furthermore, I've also had the opportunity to work with UCLA's Division 1 Women's Water Polo program, helping the Bruins win the National Collegiate Athletic Association National Championship in 2024. Being part of competitive team environments has taught me a lot about communication, accountability, adaptability, and contributing to something bigger than yourself.

I've also taken on mentorship and leadership roles at UCLA. Through MentorSEAS, I mentor first year engineering students and help introduce them to engineering opportunities and the broader engineering community. Outside of engineering, I've held leadership responsibilities in my fraternity as well, serving as the Grievance Chairmen, settling internal disputes and providing advice to those who seek it. Additionally, with Men's Club Water Polo, I helped create a developmental team for the program, allowing an additional 19 students to play in games and travel to tournaments, serving as their inaugural coach.

Those experiences have influenced how I approach engineering. I like solving technical problems, but I also like working with people, hearing different perspectives, and figuring things out together.
